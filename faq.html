Frequently Asked Questions

How do I install the EA?

1. Open MetaTrader 4
2. Go to File → Open Data Folder → MQL4 → Experts
3. Copy the EA file into this folder
4. Restart MetaTrader
5. The EA will appear in the Navigator window

Do I need to configure anything?

The EA is ready to work with default settings. You can additionally adjust StopLoss, TakeProfit and RiskPercent.

Which timeframe is recommended?

Recommended timeframes are M1 and M5.

What is the recommended minimum deposit?

Recommended minimum deposit: from $100.

Which instruments are recommended?

Recommended instrument: XAUUSD (Gold).

Which account types are supported?

ECN, Standard and Pro accounts.

Who is BesterScalper designed for?

For traders interested in automated trading, high-frequency trading and multi-symbol strategies.

Does the EA include risk management?

Yes. Every trade includes stop-loss protection and configurable risk management.
